The rules

With be, swap the verb and the subject. With other verbs, put do or does before the subject.

Questions with beVerb + Subject
Areyoutired?
With a question wordQuestion + Verb + Subject

Other verbs: ASIAuxiliary + Subject + Infinitive
Doyouwork from home?
Other verbs: QuASIQuestion + Auxiliary + Subject + Infinitive

question wordverb be or auxiliarysubject
Watch out: use does for he, she, it and one person (your boss, Anna). The main verb has no -s: Does she like coffee? not likes.
